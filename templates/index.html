Physical & Ecological Mechanics
1. Temperature & Humidity (>50%)

Regulate vapor pressure deficit. Hot air and dry humidity desiccate fine fuels into combustible kindling.

2. Wind Speed

Bends flames forward to preheat unburnt fuel ahead and lofts burning embers over long distances.

3. NDVI & Terrain Slope

Low NDVI indicates senescent dry biomass. Slopes over 15° create an upward chimney effect, accelerating spread rate.
